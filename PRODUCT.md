# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Web Awesome (UI components and design tokens) with Build Awesome (static site generator). Static output, with no server-side app. Deploy target not decided yet.

## Users

A general audience: anyone who comes across the owner's name or links, such as peers, potential collaborators, and curious readers. They come to see what the owner has made and written. They aren't there to buy anything or to log in.

## Product Purpose

Earlmade is Cody's personal site (confirm the public display name before using it). It is a public record of Cody's design and development projects and writing, plus a short about and contact section. A visit succeeds when the visitor sees the work. Reaching out is welcome, but it isn't the goal the site is built around.

## Positioning

The site's claim is the work itself. What sets it apart is the specific projects and writing, not a pitch. "Earlmade" is a handle with no backstory that the site needs to explain.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sections: design/dev projects, writing/blog, about, contact.
- No commerce, accounts, or app behaviour.
- Undecided: the deploy target, the domain, whether projects get full case studies or short entries, and the contact method (email, form, or social links).

## Evidence on Hand

None yet. There are no project write-ups, posts, photos, logo, or testimonials in the repo. Future work must not invent projects, clients, results, quotes, or bio facts. Use clearly marked placeholders until real content arrives.

## Product Principles

1. The work leads. Every page exists to get a visitor to a project or a piece of writing.
2. Show, don't pitch. No sales language or claims the content can't back up.
3. Low upkeep. Adding a post or project should mean adding a file, not editing templates.
4. Durable and fast. Static, standards-based, and readable without JavaScript where possible.
